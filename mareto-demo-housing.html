Safety & Risk Analysis

7 incidents over the reporting period averages 1.4 per week. With 4 occupied beds, this means roughly 1 incident per 3 resident-weeks — an elevated rate requiring attention.

57% of incidents required emergency services response. This is high and suggests need for enhanced on-site de-escalation capacity and conflict resolution programming.

Resident-on-resident violence (fights + physical assault) accounts for 57% of all incidents. Room assignment strategy, supervised common areas, and structured programming during high-risk hours (evening shifts) should be priorities.

42% intake safety flags compared to 100% incident-free bed checks and wellness checks shows that while intake populations carry risk factors, shelter operations protocols are maintaining safety standards during monitoring periods.

Review de-escalation protocols after 3 high-severity incidents. Schedule refresher training for evening shift staff.

Follow up on 10 "partially diverted" cases to verify alternative supports are holding.

Complete safety plans for the 1 diversion-impossible (safety) case and all residents with risk flags.

Short-Term (30 Days)

Strategic (90 Days)
