Executive Summary

Over 7 months, the program has served 33 people with 22 cases across three programs. The caseload is characterized by high domestic violence prevalence (70% of risk factors), significant Indigenous representation (38% of intakes), and strong direct service engagement (78% of hours). The system demonstrates effective intake-to-program alignment and culturally grounded service delivery. Key gaps include care plan documentation (27% coverage), reportable circumstance volume in Project Parent (67% of all reports), and the need for specialized sexual exploitation response pathways. The data supports a strategic focus on three priorities: safety infrastructure, documentation compliance, and culturally responsive capacity building.

Immediate: This Month

Complete care plans for all 11 cases without documented plans. prioritize high-risk and DV-involved cases.

Verify all 9 reportable circumstances are filed within CFCSA legislated timelines and documented in the system.

Review the 2 undefined sexual exploitation screenings and complete assessments.

Ensure the single high-risk case has a current, reviewed safety plan with supervisor sign-off.

Short-Term: Next Quarter

Implement DV-informed practice training for all workers given 70% prevalence in risk assessments.

Build specialized referral pathways for the 38% sexual exploitation screening rate.

Establish a supervisory review cadence for Project Parent given it carries 67% of reportable circumstances.

Improve Indigenous identity data capture. move the 13% unspecified responses to under 5%.

Strategic: 6–12 Months

Develop early intervention referral partnerships to reduce police-initiated intakes (currently 33%) in favour of community-based referrals.

Establish case duration benchmarks. only 2 closures in 7 months may indicate caseload accumulation risk.

Expand culturally grounded service locations. Elder's Lodge and Cultural Centre events should grow proportionally with caseload.

Build a data quality framework: standardize fields, reduce undefined values, and ensure assessments are consistently completed.

Mareto Help Center

Welcome to Mareto

A two-minute orientation for your first day at Greenfield Family Centre. These are the handful of places you'll live in, and a first path to try before you serve a real family.

The key areas you'll use most

Almost everything you do happens in one of these.

Person Registry

The record for everyone you serve — demographics, characteristics, and who needs support.

Intake

The intake form a person submits to request assistance, linked to a program and their needs.

Case Management

Active case files for each person. Track goals, notes, referrals, and case status.

Programs

Family Support, Youth Services, Counselling, Employment, Early Learning — all your programs.

Reports

Funder reports, strategic insights, real-time analytics — your impact, ready to share.

Shelter & Housing

Bed management, incident reports, resources — everything for shelter operations.

Try this first-day path

Walk it once with a practice family and the real thing will feel easy.

1

Open Person Registry and create a practice New Family with a few household members.

2

Go to Intake, choose New Assessment, select your family, and pick a program.

3

Open a Case — add a goal or two, write an initial case note.

4

Open Reports and check the Report Generator to see how data flows through.

5

Visit the Event Log and log a quick interaction note.

You're ready. That's the whole loop in miniature. When you're set for the details, the Staff Handbook walks a real family end to end, and Feedback lets you tell us what's working.

Questions on your first day? Use Feedback inside Mareto — it stays inside your own system.

The working guide to Mareto at Greenfield Family Centre. It follows the day as it actually runs: a person arrives, an intake is completed, a case is opened, services are delivered.

Person Registry

Every individual your organization serves lives here.

Creating a New Person

Click + Add at the top of the Person Registry list. Fill in last name, first name, date of birth, and contact information. Save to create the record.

Family Groupings

Navigate to Family under Person Registry. Link individuals into family units. Children are always tied to a parent or guardian record.

Searching Records

Use the search bar at the top of the list. Search by last name, first name, phone number, or any combination. Results update as you type.

Relationships

Open the Relationship tab to define connections between people — partner, sibling, emergency contact, caseworker assignment.

Privacy note: Person records contain sensitive information. Only users with the correct role permissions can view full records. Check User Access if you need to adjust who sees what.

Case Management

Cases are the core of service delivery. Each case tracks one person's journey through your programs.

Opening a Case

From a completed intake, click Open Case. Select the program, assign a caseworker, and set an initial goal. The case appears in the active list immediately.

Case Notes

Every interaction gets a note. Click into the case, select Add Note, record what happened, and save. Notes are timestamped and tied to the caseworker.

Goals & Outcomes

Set measurable goals at case opening. Update progress over time. When a goal is met, mark it complete — this feeds directly into funder reports.

Closing a Case

When services are complete, update the case status to Closed. Add a closing summary and outcome. Closed cases remain searchable for audits and reporting.

Privacy & Sessions

How Mareto protects client information and manages user sessions.

Session timeout: For privacy, a session that sits idle logs out after a period and shows a countdown first. This is a safeguard on client information, not a fault — just sign back in to pick up where you left off.

User Access & Roles

Admins control who sees what. Role-based permissions determine access to records, modules, and reports. Review roles in User Access.

Every action is logged — who viewed, edited, or exported a record and when. Audit logs are available to admin users for compliance reviews.

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Client information never leaves your organization's secure environment.

Track consent forms at the person level. Each record shows whether consent is on file, when it was signed, and when it expires.

Words We Use

A quick reference for the terms you'll see throughout Mareto. If a word on screen doesn't match anything here, check with your admin.

Person — any individual in the system: a client, caregiver, child, or community member. The base record everything else links to.
Family — a group of linked person records. Children belong to their family and are not listed separately in the Person Registry.
Relationship — a defined connection between two people: partner, sibling, emergency contact, caseworker, or custom.
Intake — the process of receiving a new request for services. An intake creates a record that can become a case.
Assessment — a scored evaluation completed during intake. Includes acuity, stability, engagement, and transition tools.
Waitlist — the queue of intakes waiting to be assigned to a program or caseworker.
Case — an active service file for one person. Tracks goals, notes, referrals, and status from open to close.
Case Note — a timestamped record of an interaction, observation, or action taken on a case. Tied to the caseworker who wrote it.
Program — a service your organization delivers: Family Support, Youth Services, Counselling, Employment, Early Learning, or custom.
Enrollment — when a person is added to a program. One person can be enrolled in multiple programs at once.
Event — any logged interaction: a phone call, home visit, group session, or crisis response. Found in the Event Log.
Referral — a formal request to connect a person with another service, either within your organization (internal) or to a partner agency (external).
Resource — a physical asset managed in the Shelter module: a bed, a unit, a room. Tracked on the kanban board.
Incident Report — a record of a safety or behavioural event in a shelter or program setting. Linked to involved persons and follow-up actions.
Daily Log — shift-by-shift notes for shelter operations. Handoff details, observations, and anything the next shift needs to know.
Attendance — check-in tracking for programs or shelter stays. Records who was present, meal counts, and daily occupancy.
Funder Contract — an agreement with a funding body. Tracks dollar amount, reporting schedule, and compliance deadlines.
Report Generator — the tool for building funder-ready reports. Select date range, program, and format, then export.
Strategic Insights — high-level dashboards showing trends, service gaps, and outcome trajectories across all programs.
Reports and Analytics — the live reporting studio with KPI cards, time-series charts, and data exports.
Wellness Tracker — a module for recording wellness scores and health indicators over time. Used in clinical and shelter settings.
Timesheet — staff hours logged per day. Includes clock-in/out, break tracking, and leave requests.
Staff Leave — vacation, sick, or personal time requests submitted through the Timesheets module.
User Access — the admin panel for managing who can see and do what. Role-based permissions control visibility of records and modules.
Namespace — the top-level container for your organization in Mareto. All data, users, and configuration live inside your namespace.
Survey — a configurable questionnaire sent to clients or staff. Responses feed into reporting and program evaluation.
Outreach — field-based work tracked by location and contact. Logs visits, interactions, and GPS-tagged notes.
Appointment — a scheduled meeting or session. Appears on the calendar and can be linked to a case or program.
Status — the current state of a record: Active, Pending, Closed, Approved, or In Progress. Every list can be filtered by status.
Audit Trail — the system log of who viewed, edited, or exported a record and when. Available to admin users.
