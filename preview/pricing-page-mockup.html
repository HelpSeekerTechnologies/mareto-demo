Included in every plan

Everything you need to run your programs and report to your funders.

Every plan is set up around your programs and the way your team works. This is what you get as standard, before anything you add.

Client records

One record per person across every program, with profiles and demographics, duplicate checking, and family and household links.

Intake and referrals

Online forms and program registration, intake forms for staff, a referral and intake queue, and consent recorded on the person’s record.

Case work

Case files and plans for each program, case notes, document upload, and assessments and outcomes tracked over time.

Scheduling and programs

Appointments and staff calendars, classes, groups and attendance, waitlists, and reminders and automations.

Reporting

A report for each funder you name during setup, live dashboards, and exports to Excel and PDF.

Privacy and security

Hosted in Canada, access by role down to individual fields, two-step login, and audit logs of every change.

Setup and support

Built around your programs and forms, three rounds of changes before you go live, training for your team, and our help desk.

No surprises

What you will never be charged for

Adding more clients or records. Updates and security patches. Exporting your data, any time. Leaving: there are no exit fees.

Renewal increases are capped at 4 percent a year, in writing.
